        Controls
        

          W A S DMove
          SPACEJump (hold to keep jumping)
          CTRL / double-tap WSprint
          SHIFTSneak
          MOUSELook around
          LEFT / RIGHT CLICKBreak / place a block
          MIDDLE CLICKPick the block you are looking at
          1–9 / scrollSelect a hotbar slot
          SHIFT + scrollZoom the third-person camera
          EInventory (9 × 3 grid)
          VCycle view: first person → third person → front
          FFullscreen
          GDebug screen (position, speed, FPS)
          ESCRelease the mouse / pause
        

        

          📱 iPad with a Magic Keyboard: click to grab the mouse, Esc releases it. If Safari
          refuses to lock the pointer the game switches to free look automatically — moving the
          cursor turns the view and the screen edges keep turning. Going fullscreen usually makes
          the real lock work.

          👆 Touch only: left thumb steers, right thumb looks, on-screen buttons do the rest.

          💾 Your world saves itself in this browser.
